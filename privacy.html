Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

autopost ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial tool used only by its owner to schedule and publish the owner's own short videos to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the connected channel (youtube.upload scope). The app sends videos, titles, descriptions, tags and schedule settings. It does not read viewers' data, comments, analytics or other Google account data.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (user.info.basic) and permission to publish videos (video.publish) to the connected account, plus the publishing options TikTok returns for that account.
  • Instagram: basic profile information (account ID and username) and permission to publish content (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish) to the connected professional account.

How data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to publish the owner's videos at the times the owner chooses.
  • Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and in a local file on the owner's computer. They are never published or shared.
  • The app keeps a record of each post's status, IDs and links in the owner's private repository. It does not store any data about other people.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Data goes only to the platform it belongs to (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta/Instagram) and GitHub, which runs the app.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google/YouTube: Google security settings → Third-party access.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites.

Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless. To delete stored tokens and post records, or for any privacy question, contact velvetfig130@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 30 days.
